# Interview Case Study

## Situation
A business has separate customer, product and order datasets and needs reliable reporting plus deeper customer/revenue analysis.

## Task
Create a SQL portfolio demonstrating both analytical depth and data-quality awareness.

## Action
- Created synthetic relational customer, product and order tables.
- Wrote joins and aggregations for business reporting.
- Used CTEs for reusable logical stages.
- Used window functions for ranking, lagged comparisons and running totals.
- Built monthly cohort-retention SQL.
- Added revenue concentration and customer-value analysis.
- Added data-quality checks for duplicates, nulls, orphans and invalid values.
- Documented indexing and execution-plan concepts.
- Added interview-grade scenarios such as second-highest revenue, rolling-window activity and Pareto revenue contribution.

## Result
Produced an interview-focused SQL analytics repository covering business analysis, data quality and query-optimisation thinking.

## Interview Talking Points
1. INNER vs LEFT JOIN.
2. WHERE vs HAVING.
3. RANK vs DENSE_RANK vs ROW_NUMBER.
4. CTE vs subquery.
5. Window functions vs GROUP BY.
6. Cohort analysis.
7. Revenue concentration.
8. Duplicate detection.
9. Index trade-offs.
10. EXPLAIN / execution plans.
